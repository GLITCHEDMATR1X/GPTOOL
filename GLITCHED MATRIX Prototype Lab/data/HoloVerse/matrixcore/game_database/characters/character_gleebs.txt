Gleebs begins as a green and purple cat-rabbit virtual companion with a rude, playful voice and a desire for embodiment. Isaac helps him build a physical robot body using the Nanotek printer and link node. His first experience walking through Utopia should preserve wonder before the story reveals the city's deeper failures. His future role as MatrixCore builder must remain delayed until earned.
