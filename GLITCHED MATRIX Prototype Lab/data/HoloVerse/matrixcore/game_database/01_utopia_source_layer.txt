The Utopia Project is treated as the recovered old-world chronicle: the record of what happened before MatrixCore existed. MatrixCore is what survived after that world broke. HoloVerse is the playable reconstruction layer that lets the player explore safe fragments of that past through regions, bots, artifacts, and archive unlocks.
