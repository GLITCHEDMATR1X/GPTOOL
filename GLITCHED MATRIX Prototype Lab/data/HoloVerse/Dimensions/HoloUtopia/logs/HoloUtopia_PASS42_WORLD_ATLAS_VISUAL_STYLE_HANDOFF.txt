HoloUtopia Pass 42 - World Atlas Visual Style

Goal:
- Make the actual Panda3D game view move toward the generated neon city-map reference.
- Show all 9 authored districts as a coherent world atlas.
- Correct the central Simulation/Core so it reads as a substantial hub/plaza instead of a thin line.
- Keep all changes visual-only and collisionless.

Changed files:
- data/HoloUtopia/holoutopia_city_world_style.py
- data/HoloUtopia/holoutopia_game_runtime.py
- data/HoloUtopia/holoutopia_town_blocks.py

- data/database/utopia/runtime/holoutopia_runtime_bridge.json

What changed:
1) Added city-atlas style layer
- Adds district pads under all 9 districts.
- Adds dark global ring roads and radial roads between districts.
- Adds faint district-color conduit lines for theme identity.
- Adds subtle district frames/corner ticks.

2) Central Simulation/Core hub rebuilt visually
- Added solid layered circular plaza surfaces.
- Added concentric raised rings.
- Added radial spokes.
- Added central pyramid/core glyph and vertical beam.
- Added small wall/tick columns around the hub.
- Central portal plaza block now stays a low floor/plaza footprint so it does not render as a building on top of the core.

3) Cleaner world-atlas proof screenshots
- Added world_atlas and world_atlas_close screenshot camera views.
- Screenshot proof views remove Watch Mode/status panels so the city can be inspected without UI obstruction.
- District labels are hidden in integrated city runtime views to avoid label clutter.

4) Safety
- No collision geometry added.
- No HoloVerse routing changes.
- No HoloCore changes.
- No artifact routing changes.
- No population, schedule, commute, watch, or citizen AI rule changes.
