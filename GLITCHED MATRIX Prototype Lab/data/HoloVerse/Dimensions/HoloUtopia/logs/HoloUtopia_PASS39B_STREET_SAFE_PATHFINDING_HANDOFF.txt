HoloUtopia Pass 39B - Street-Safe Citizen Pathfinding

Scope:
- Improve visible population pathfinding after Simulation Hub activity routing.
- Keep Residential as the home/garrison district.
- Let citizens garrison inside home buildings virtually.
- Prevent visible citizens from standing on building footprints/roofs.

Implementation notes:
- Added pure-Python street/activity-pad placement resolver.
- Visible virtual citizens snap to authored roads, portal pads, public pads, or safe plaza/garden surfaces.
- Home/building occupancy remains virtual and is represented by garrison counts.
- Building footprint exclusion uses town block metadata and avoids non-walkable blocks.
- Added caching to avoid expensive JSON/sample recomputation during Panda3D frames.
- Lowered same-node spread because pathfinding now handles crowd distribution safely.
