HoloUtopia Pass 40 - Watch Mode Foundation

Goal alignment:
- Return to the original design goal: a living AI city that can be watched over now and joined later.
- This pass adds read-only watch/director information without changing routing, collision, population caps, or HoloVerse artifact behavior.

Changed / added files:
- data/HoloUtopia/holoutopia_watch_mode.py
- data/HoloUtopia/holoutopia_game_runtime.py

- data/database/utopia/runtime/holoutopia_runtime_bridge.json

What changed:
1) Added Watch Mode snapshot system
- Builds a compact city oversight snapshot from the live simulation frame.
- Tracks clock, population, visible representatives, activity cap, phase, average energy, district focus, flow state, cluster distribution, and future join target.

2) Added Watch Mode 3D panel
- Collisionless 3D panel in the integrated Panda3D city view.
- Shows readable director/watch information while still seeing the city.
- The panel is read-only and does not write logs or authored data.

3) Preserved live-city rules
- Residential remains the home/garrison district.
- Central Core Civic Ring remains the Simulation Hub.
- Citizens remain little 3D people with no circles and no name labels.
- Visible citizens remain street-safe and cannot stand on roofs/building footprints.
- Garrisoned citizens remain virtual inside homes.

4) Join-mode foundation only
- Watch snapshot reports a future join target, but join mode is not activated yet.
- No player-control or possession system was added in this pass.
