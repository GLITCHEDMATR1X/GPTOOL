HoloUtopia Pass 39E - Staggered Citizen Schedules

Goal:
- Keep citizens from building up in the same Simulation Hub activity areas.
- Preserve the Residential garrison model: all citizens live in Residential and only visible representatives are rendered outside.
- Preserve no-standing-on-buildings pathing from Pass 39B.

What changed:
1) Added schedule staggering data
- New simulation_hub_schedule_staggering.json defines 20 unique visible schedule profiles, personal schedule offsets, schedule archetypes, and a per-cluster distribution guard.
- The distribution guard caps visible representatives to 4 per activity cluster.

2) Added runtime schedule staggering module
- holoutopia_schedule_staggering.py builds deterministic personal schedule profiles for the 20 visible Simulation Hub representatives.
- Each representative receives unique fields such as schedule_profile_id, personal_clock, personal_day_offset_minutes, work_start, work_end, activity_start, and return_home.

3) Improved Simulation Hub routing
- district_activity_rules.json now has multiple work/activity destinations instead of one work pile-up point.
- Representatives are distributed across pod, console, forum, reader, arrival, social sync, and cooldown clusters.
- Existing activity behavior poses and street-safe pathfinding are preserved.

4) Added validator
- validate_holoutopia_schedule_staggering.py checks that sample clocks maintain 20 visible representatives, at least 18 unique profiles, at least 12 personal offsets, spread across at least 5 clusters, and no cluster has more than 4 representatives.

Validation highlights:
- min_unique_profiles=20
- max_cluster_population=4
- 08:30, 12:45, and 18:15 all spread citizens across multiple Simulation Hub clusters.

No changes:
- No HoloVerse routing changes.
- No artifact routing changes.
- No HoloCore changes.
- No collision changes.
- No increase to the visible outdoor population cap.
- No 1000 actor spawn.
