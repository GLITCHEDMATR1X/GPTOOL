HoloUtopia Pass 47A - Citizen Dialogue Foundation

Scope:
- Adds brief proximity-only citizen speech bubbles.
- Adds question/answer dialogue categories using live runtime state.
- Keeps bubbles small, capped, and anchor-spaced so they do not stack over each other.
- Keeps citizens silent while garrisoned/home-hidden.
- Keeps all changes visual/runtime-only with no authored city data writes.

Changed/added files:
- data/HoloUtopia/holoutopia_citizen_dialogue.py

- data/database/utopia/dialogue/question_answer_catalog.json
- data/database/utopia/runtime/holoutopia_runtime_bridge.json

Dialogue rules:
- Citizens only speak when another visible citizen is within proximity radius.
- Garrisoned/private-home citizens do not speak.
- One citizen can only participate in one active bubble at a time.
- Active bubbles are capped at 3.
- Bubble anchors are spaced apart to prevent overlap.
- Question/answer lines are short and answer from runtime state.
- Unknown or unsupported questions fail safely with an honest unknown answer.
