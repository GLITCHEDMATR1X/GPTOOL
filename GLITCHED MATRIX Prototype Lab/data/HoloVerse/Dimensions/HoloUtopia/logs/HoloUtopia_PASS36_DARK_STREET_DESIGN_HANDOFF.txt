HoloUtopia Pass 36 — Dark Street Design

Scope:
- Added plain dark-grey street surfaces for authored radial and ring roads.
- Added subtle curb/cap edge treatment only; no traffic lanes, center lines, or painted markings.
- Added same street-surface pass to neighborhood overlays so close-up residential views read as actual streets instead of only grid lines.
- Kept district building color/accent themes from Pass 35 intact.

Implementation notes:

- Road rendering is additive and renderer-only; no authored data or AI schedule data changed.
- Street slabs render before buildings/foundations so foundations cover street overlaps cleanly.
- Ring roads are approximated with small dark surface segments plus inner/outer curb loops.
