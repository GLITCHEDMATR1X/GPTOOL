HoloUtopia Pass 35 - District-Themed Multicolor Accents

Scope:
- Add multicolor visual accents while keeping each district inside its own color identity.
- Continue from Pass 34 stylized structures.
- Keep the change visual-only and conservative.

Changed file:

What changed:
1) Deterministic theme-safe accent system
- Added stable SHA-based accent selection.
- Added per-district accent palettes.
- Accents are deterministic per building/kit, not random per launch.

2) District palettes
- Residential: emerald, cyan, soft-magenta, warm garden highlights.
- Harbor: aqua, deep blue, teal, pale water highlights.
- Industrial: lime, amber, cyan utility, orange service accents.
- Market: magenta, gold, cyan, violet.
- Archive: violet, blue, pale memory-cyan, pink-violet.
- Security: green, red, yellow, cyan status accents.
- Civic/Central: blue, cyan-white, gold, red core accents.
- Glitched/Quarantine: red, green, cyan, amber warning accents.

3) New accent geometry
- Added lobby bands, side bands, roof bands, and roof ticks.
- Recolored existing facade frames, spines, crowns, portals, balconies, masts, rings, pipes, and signage through theme palettes.
- Kept building faces opaque by default and transparent only when selected/cutaway.
