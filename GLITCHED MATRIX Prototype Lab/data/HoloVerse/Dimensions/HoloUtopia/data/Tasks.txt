Game Rules


Timescale: 24hr ingame clock, 1 earth minute = 1 game hour.


// Residential Building Garrison \\


smallest to largest = 1x population multiplied x2

- Citizens

All citizens of HoloUtopia will live in residential district.

Citizen energy 100% lasts 15 game hours, return to garrison by foot and remain inside of their home building for 9 game hours to fully recharge.

daily motives = work, activities, replenish
